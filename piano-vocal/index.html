Before you send it

A few common questions.

This type of production is often about quality, taste and musical judgement rather than a huge number of layers. Here are a few practical questions artists often have.

Can the production stay very simple?

Yes. The arrangement can stay intimate and restrained if that serves the song best. Simplicity can be a strength when the details are handled well.

Do you only work with acoustic piano?

No. We can build the song around a strong digital piano / keyboard sound as well, which is often a practical and musical fit for this kind of production.

Can you add just a little more around the piano?

Yes. Bass, subtle strings, pads, harmonies or light rhythm can be added carefully to give the song more width and movement without changing its identity.

Is this suitable for singers who perform live?

Absolutely. This path works especially well for artists whose musical identity is already rooted in live singing and emotionally direct performance.

Can I record my vocals at home?

Yes. We can guide you remotely so you can capture clean, useful takes and send the files to the studio.

What if I already have a demo or rough production?

Send it over. We can either refine what you already have or recommend a better approach if the song needs a clearer piano and vocal direction.
