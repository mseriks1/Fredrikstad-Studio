Hardware synth FAQ

Before you
send the session.

The service can stay very simple: send the musical information and a reference, agree the sound and scope, then receive the recorded audio.

Do I need to use Logic?

No. You can work in any DAW. Send a standard MIDI file together with the tempo and a reference mix or audio stem so the part can be placed correctly.

Can you keep my exact MIDI performance?

Yes. For MIDI-to-hardware work, the point is to preserve the notes and timing you created while replacing the virtual instrument with a real hardware recording.

Do I have to choose the synth myself?

No. Choose a specific instrument if you already know what you want, or describe the sound and role and we can suggest a suitable hardware option.

Can I get both raw and processed versions?

Yes. The session can be delivered raw, processed, or as both versions depending on what you want to do in the mix.

Can you create the synth part for me?

Yes. Book a custom synth session and send the track, reference and direction. Bass, pads, leads, arpeggios, textures and additional layers can be created around the production.

How do I send the MIDI file?

Start with the form below and include a demo or private link if useful. MIDI, stems and other project files are arranged directly after the first contact.
