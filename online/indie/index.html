Solo artist or band

The song can start small.
The record does not have to.

You do not need to arrive with a complete band arrangement. A solo artist can start with a simple song and build a fuller production around it. A band can send existing recordings and use the studio for production development, additional parts, mixing and finishing.

The goal is the same in both cases: create a record with energy, character and enough musical detail to feel alive without overcrowding what made the song work.

Real guitar and bass parts, programmed drums, keys and production can create a convincing band feel around the song.

Before you send it

A few common questions.

You do not need a finished band arrangement before getting in touch. The first review is about the song, what you already have and what kind of record you want to make.

I am a solo artist. Can you build a band arrangement around my song?

Yes. That is one of the main ideas behind this production path. You can begin with a voice memo, guitar/vocal, piano/vocal or rough demo and develop a fuller guitar-driven arrangement from there.

Do I need to record guitars myself?

No. Guitar and bass parts can be created as part of the production when appropriate, or we can work with tracks you have already recorded.

Can you work with an existing band recording?

Yes. Send the current mix or multitracks. We can look at production direction, editing, additional parts, vocal work, mixing and mastering depending on what the song needs.

Is this only for rock?

No. The page is aimed at indie, alternative, guitar-pop, pop-rock and other song-driven music where guitars and a band feel are part of the sound.

Can the production still include synths and electronic elements?

Yes. Indie and alternative productions often combine organic and electronic elements. Keys, synth textures and production details can be used where they support the identity of the song.

Can I record my vocals remotely?

Yes. We can provide practical guidance for remote vocal recording and then integrate the vocal into the production at Fredrikstad Studio.
