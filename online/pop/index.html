Build around the artist

Modern pop can move
in many directions.

Pop can be electronic, organic, guitar-driven, dance-oriented, intimate, cinematic or somewhere between several of those worlds. The strongest direction is the one that makes the artist and song feel most convincing.

That is why the process starts with your voice, references, writing and goals — then uses arrangement, instruments, sound design and vocal production to create a release that feels cohesive rather than generic.

The goal is not to sound like everyone else. It is to make your version of pop feel fully realised.

The vocal is part of the production

Make the voice
carry the record.

In modern pop, the lead vocal is often the most recognisable part of the production. The arrangement, key, dynamics and sound choices should make room for it — while doubles, harmonies, adlibs and effects can add width, emotion and identity.

If you record remotely, Fredrikstad Studio can also guide you through the practical recording process so the final vocal files give the production what it needs.

Before you send it

A few common questions.

A rough idea is enough for the first review. The production approach can be decided after we hear the song and understand what you want to achieve.

What if I only have lyrics or a melody?

That can be enough to begin. Song development can include lyrics, melody, topline, harmony and structure before the full production is built.

Can you produce from a simple voice memo?

Yes. A basic recording can communicate the song well enough for the initial review. We can then discuss arrangement, sound and the best next step.

Do you only make electronic pop?

No. Pop can use acoustic instruments, guitars, piano, synths, programmed elements or a combination. The sound is chosen around the artist and song.

Can you improve a production I already made?

Yes. Existing productions can be developed through arrangement changes, additional instruments, sound design, vocal production, editing, mixing or mastering.

Can I record vocals at home?

Yes. We can provide practical remote guidance for recording clean vocal takes and preparing the files for production and mixing.

Can we work on several singles or an EP?

Yes. A project can begin with one song and grow into several releases. Working together over time also makes it easier to develop a recognisable artist sound.
